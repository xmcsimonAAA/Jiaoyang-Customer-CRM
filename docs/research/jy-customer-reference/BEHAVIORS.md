# Interaction Behaviors

- Navigation changes the active workspace without a full page reload.
- Dashboard cards open the relevant filtered customer workflow.
- Customer rows open a detail drawer; editable cells save on change.
- New customer and follow-up flows open focused modal forms.
- Mobile keeps customer list, new customer, and follow-up as persistent bottom actions.
- Custom columns, imports, exports, assignment, and merge controls remain permission-gated.
